# Third-party components

- **godot-cpp**, MIT, pinned by `godot-cpp.ref`; linked into the native library. Its copyright/license notice is shipped in `addons/ffb/licenses/godot-cpp.txt`.
- **gd-plug 0.2.6**, MIT, imjp94 and contributors, commit `963f9d9958ad51fdb5c98a1a08db09a389b62c3f`. The original script and license under `tests/vendor/gd-plug/` are used only for offline package-installation tests; gd-plug is not bundled inside the FFB addon.
- **Godot Engine**, MIT, downloaded from official Godot releases into build/test container images. Godot is not included in the addon ZIP.
